Jumping Robot

My favorite project I've ever cretaed so far. I devloped this project over the course of fall semester and winter break of my 2nd year of community college. The main inspiration for this project is from my favorite game of all time, Rainbow Six Siege. In that game, there are two sides, attackers and defenders. Before the start of every round attackers are allowed to scope out the map and find the site where defenders are located. To do this, they have access to a two-wheeled jumping drone with a camera embedded into it. My project has the exact same functions, and like the game, can be controlled by a phone.

The main mechanism to drive the robot is similar to that of an airsoft gun.
